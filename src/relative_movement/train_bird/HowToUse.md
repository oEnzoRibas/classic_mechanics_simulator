## How to Use

1. Run the program:

       python passaroOFC.py

2. Click **Start** to begin the simulation. The trains move toward each other while the bird flies back and forth between them.

3. Click **Pause** to stop the animation. Click **Start** again to continue.

4. Click **Reset** to return the simulation to its initial state.

5. Choose a display mode:
   - **Visual only** shows the 3D animation.
   - **Visual + graph** shows the animation and the position-versus-time graph.

6. Choose a camera view:
   - **Side view** shows the scene from the side.
   - **Top view** shows it from above.

7. Use the **Curves** menu to choose which objects appear on the graph: Train A, Train B, the bird, or a combination of them.

8. To change the speeds, enter a value in km/h for Train A, Train B, and the bird, then click **Apply speeds**. The simulation resets with the new values and remains paused until you click **Start**.

   The bird must be faster than both trains to reach each one and fly back toward the other. If a value is invalid, the program displays an error message.

9. Click **Original values** to restore the speeds from the original problem: 30 km/h for each train and 60 km/h for the bird.

10. Open the flashcard to reveal the original problem and its solution one step at a time. Click **Restart flashcard** to close the revealed steps and try solving it again
